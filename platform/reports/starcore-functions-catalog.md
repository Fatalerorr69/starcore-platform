- **`snapshot_list()`** — List snapshots of a Proxmox VM or LXC container.
- **`snapshot_delete()`** — Delete a snapshot of a Proxmox VM or LXC container.
- **`snapshot_rollback()`** — Roll back a Proxmox VM or LXC container to a snapshot. Discards current state.
